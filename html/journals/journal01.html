Introduction

"Archive" is not only a collection of my digital works. It is also a record of inner voices in mind throughout everyday.

Writing down own thoughts is one of the most effective ways to organise them and deepen into the core of matter.

Sometimes, I feel something amiss on social values deemed to be normal or trends spreading in real world.

However, sharing individual thinking in public could be problematic, a target to be attacked, or unintentionally offensive to a particular group of people, which is ironically a serious consequence for over-socialising in this digital society.

That is why I created this webpage, "Archive".

Here, social validation and peer-review are not intended. Since this is my personal collection of everything, whether or not you will visit here is your decision.

For this reason, I do not care about it even if some viewers find my journals inappropriate.

Of course, I am genuinely happy to welcome those of you being curious to explore my gallarey.

Origin

I was born and raised in Osaka, Japan.

Both of my parents are actually from Vietnam and immigrated to Japan when they were young. So, I have grown up being exposed to a multi different culture between Japan and Vietnam.

Despite this family background, I consider myself Japanese, as the Japanese-environment around me has shaped my character, values, and perspectives.

Creativity

Creativity is potential self-expression.

I have been by this idea since young, specifically obsessed with motion graphics such as kinetic typography, animated lyric video, and dynamic scene transition especially seen in advertisements.

The idea behind this is I can visually actualise what I imagine in my head.

Therefore, I started to edit visual contents and learn programming.
