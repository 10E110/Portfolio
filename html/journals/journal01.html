Introduction

"Archive" does not only store digital works but also inner voices in mind about every moment.

Writing down own thoughts is the most effective approach to organise and deepen into the core of matter.

Sometimes, I feel something amiss on social values deemed to be normal or trends spreading in real world.

However, sharing individual thinking in public could be problematic or unintentionally offend a particular group of people, which is ironically a serious consequence for over-socialising in this digital society .

That is why I created this webpage, "Archive".

Here, social validation and peer-review are not intended. Since this is my personal collection of everything, whether or not you will visit here is your decision.

For this reason, I do not care about it even if some viewers find my journals inappropriate.

Of course, I am genuinely happy to welcome those of you being curious to explore this site.
