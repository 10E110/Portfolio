Promotional Video for English-focused and IB Course of Kindai University High School

2026/08/21

Overview

This promotional video was created on 2024/06/05 in order to advertise English-focused course(Eigotokka) and IB course of Kindai University High School, directed by my fellow classmates: Shuya Yokomichi & Reia Nakahigashi.

The video shows the overview of English-focused course through dynamic morph animation and fast-paced scene transition with the lively BGM.

After that, a scene swithces to the overview of IB course. With gradational design based off blue colour, sophisticated atomosphere conveys intelligence, profession, and immaculacy of IB course's image.

Interviews are also in the video, and especially student interviews will help viewers get to know how the course is actually run and what kind of experience will be offered in it.
