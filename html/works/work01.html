Promotional Video for English-focused and IB Course of Kindai University High School

2026/08/24

Overview

This promotional video was created on 2024/06/05 in order to advertise English-focused course(Eigotokka) and IB course of Kindai University High School, by communicating learning environment and international perspective to prospective students.

The project was directed by my fellow classmates: Shuya Yokomichi and Reia Nakahigashi.

Design Decision

English-focused Course

As English-focused course is a student-centred classroom with the focus of interactive learning approach, dynamic morph animation and fast-paced scene transition are developed with the lively BGM that is a key element in the flow of the video.

IB Course

The colour blue universally symbolizes calm, trust, and stability. It is linked to the open sky and deep water, representing both peacefulness and depth.

Following this, the sophisticated blue-based design and the relaxed atomosphere convey intelligence, profession, and immaculacy of IB course's image.
